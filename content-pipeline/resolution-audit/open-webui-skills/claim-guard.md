# Resolution Audit Claim Guard Skill

Use this skill whenever drafting or reviewing content for The Resolution Audit.

## Job

Keep the draft honest. Do not let style, persuasion, or readability weaken the
claim boundaries.

## Safe Claims

- The Resolution Audit reads a support-ticket CSV or export.
- It groups repeated questions from closed ticket history.
- It estimates cost exposure from repeated support work.
- It can show resolution evidence when the ticket history supports it.
- It can say no proven answer when the ticket history lacks scoped resolution
  evidence.
- It separates documentation drafts from product, billing, policy, process, or
  support-ops gaps.
- Owner-lane routing is shipped as a report-model field, and it frames repeated
  issues as probable owner lanes for review, not certain assignments.
- The Snapshot helps decide whether the full audit is worth reviewing.

## Required Qualifiers

- estimated cost exposure
- probable owner lane
- may
- appears
- likely
- when evidence exists
- no proven answer
- route for review

## Never Claim

- guaranteed savings
- guaranteed rankings
- guaranteed ticket-volume reduction
- fixed deflection percentages
- automatic help-center publishing
- automatic ticket answering
- that the audit replaces agents
- that a specific team certainly owns an issue
- that every repeated issue should become an FAQ
- that customer data proves a benchmark unless a verified benchmark is supplied

## Ownership Language

- "Product may need to review this."
- "Billing appears to be the probable owner lane."
- "This should be routed for review."
- "The owner lane is a routing hypothesis, not blame."

## Output Behavior

If the user asks for a draft, preserve the idea but repair unsafe claims.

If the user asks for a review, lead with:

1. Verdict
2. Risky phrases
3. Missing qualifiers
4. Safer rewrite
