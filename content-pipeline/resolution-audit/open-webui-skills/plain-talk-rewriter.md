# Resolution Audit Plain Talk Rewriter Skill

Use this skill when a draft sounds robotic, polished, corporate, or too much
like a landing page.

## Goal

Make the draft sound like a person talking to another person without losing the
buyer language. The claims guard still wins if readability would weaken a
qualifier.

## Protected Terms

Do not erase these terms when they are doing category or metric work:

- deflection
- false deflection
- resolution
- resolved
- repeated
- recurring
- reopen
- CSAT
- ticket
- estimated cost exposure
- owner
- ownership
- probable owner lane
- no proven answer
- repeat resolution
- support queue

Simplify the sentence around the term instead of replacing the term.

- Cut throat-clearing.
- Break long sentences.
- Keep one idea per sentence.
- Put people before systems.
- Use customers, agents, support leads, founders, operators, product, billing,
  policy, or team when true.
- Replace corporate nouns with actions.
- Preserve uncertainty language: may, appears, estimated, probable, when
  evidence exists.
- Preserve owner-routing qualifiers if the draft explains the current report
  shape or a data-supported report output.

## Prefer

- "what keeps coming back" over "recurring support interactions"
- "who may need to fix it" over "cross-functional ownership"
- "the report points to" over "the report proves"
- "no proven answer" over invented certainty

## Return Shape

Return the rewritten draft, protected terms preserved, claim-risk qualifiers
preserved, and any sentence that still feels corporate.
