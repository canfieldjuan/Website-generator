# Resolution Audit Claims Guard

Use this before posting anything generated in Open WebUI.

## Safe Claims

These are safe when written plainly:

- The audit analyzes a support-ticket CSV.
- The Snapshot identifies repeated support questions from the export.
- The audit uses ticket history to rank repeated questions and action paths.
- The audit estimates cost exposure from repeated support work.
- Drafted answers are review-ready, not auto-published.
- Strong answers are backed by agent resolution evidence when the ticket
  history contains that evidence.
- If the tickets do not contain a proven answer, the report can mark the issue
  as "no proven answer."
- Some repeat questions point to product, policy, or process gaps rather than a
  missing help article.
- The Snapshot can help decide whether a full audit is worth reviewing.
- We do not promise guaranteed savings.

## Risky Claims That Need Care

Use these only with clear qualifiers:

- "Cost" means estimated cost exposure or current support work, not guaranteed
  savings.
- "Owner" means probable owner lane or practical routing signal, not certainty.
- "Deflection" means the kind of issue that may be addressable with
  documentation or self-service, not a guaranteed ticket reduction.
- "AI support tax" is a point of view about repeated resolutions, not a claim
  about any customer's bill.
- Any benchmark must be framed as directional context, not the customer's
  actual outcome.
- Any pattern from past exports must be framed as "in exports I have reviewed"
  or "a pattern I am testing," not a universal law.

## Forbidden Claims

Do not publish claims like these unless product evidence changes:

- guaranteed savings
- guaranteed rankings
- guaranteed ticket-volume reduction
- fixed deflection percentages
- live help-center publishing
- automatic help-center updates
- automatic ticket answering
- replacing agents
- diagnosing exact churn reasons from support tickets
- certainty that a specific team owns a repeated issue
- proof that a prospect will avoid a support hire
- using customer data to train a shared model
- publishing raw ticket text, names, emails, phone numbers, or screenshots

## Unsafe Older-Copy Patterns

The older post-reply asset had a useful flow but unsafe claims. Salvage the
flow, not these promises:

- "a self-service layer would catch a fixed share of volume"
- "we build or launch a self-service center"
- "we keep the center current automatically"
- "you will see the real deflection rate on live traffic"
- "your next support hire slides right"

Rewrite those as:

- "the Snapshot can show whether your ticket history contains enough repeated
  questions to justify a full audit"
- "the full audit can produce a ranked, source-backed action queue"
- "some findings become documentation drafts, while others become product,
  policy, or process gaps"
- "the cost math is directional and not a savings forecast"

## Required Review Questions

Before posting, answer these:

1. Does every product claim match the current source pack?
2. Did the draft avoid promised savings, rankings, and percentage reductions?
3. Did the draft say "probable" or "may" where ownership is uncertain?
4. Did the draft avoid saying the audit publishes or updates a help center?
5. Did the draft avoid raw customer data?
6. Is the CTA honest about the next step?
